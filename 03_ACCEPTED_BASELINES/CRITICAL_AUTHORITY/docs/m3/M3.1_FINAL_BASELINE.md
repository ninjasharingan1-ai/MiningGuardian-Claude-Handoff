## Preserved contracts

- M2 Policy owns admissibility. DecisionEvaluationRepository remains M2 decision
  persistence. M2 prediction, scoring, ranking, timestamp_iso reconstruction,
  and Decision/Action/Execution/Outcome separation remain frozen.
- M3.0 architecture remains byte-identical and authoritative.
- M3.1 RuntimeObservation retains source and nullable source-instance identity,
  value/unit, provenance, correlation, Guardian session, schema version, quality
  and freshness without inventing missing facts.
- observation_time is source recording time; ingestion_time is Guardian receipt
  time. event_time is separate and optional. M2 historical timestamp semantics
  remain untouched.
- M3.1 quality precedence and explicit source/signal freshness remain separate
  from state estimation, prediction, reward, policy and execution safety.
- M3 Runtime Evidence Persistence owns separate additive runtime tables and is
  distinct from M2 DecisionEvaluationRepository.
- M3.1 remains read-only observability. It grants no miner, GPU, pool, algorithm,
  hardware, or live autonomous execution authority.

## Accepted nonblocking limitations

- Real hardware and sustained production validation remain outstanding.
- Native NVML driver stalls cannot be forcibly timed out by the synchronous getter boundary.
- Source authentication is not cryptographic.
- Miner process incarnation may remain unknown.
- Cross-source GPU mapping may remain unknown.
- Duplicate detection depends on stable source event identity.
- Capability caches are process-local.
- The runtime storage admission budget is not an OS filesystem quota.
- Future source-specific credential fields require explicit redaction rules.

These limitations are accepted M3.1 boundaries, not unresolved implementation
failures. The final freeze manifest indexes this baseline and the acceptance;
the archive checksum is external in M3.1_FINAL_FREEZE.sha256.
