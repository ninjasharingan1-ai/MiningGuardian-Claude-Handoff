# M3.2 S4A R2-B — Missing Opportunity Reason Authority Clarification

## Status

`R2B_MISSING_OPPORTUNITY_REASON_AUTHORITY_CLARIFIED`

ACCEPTED NORMATIVE CLARIFICATION.

This clarification closes only the MethodEvidenceUse accounting reason
required when `missing_fraction` cannot bind because no accepted opportunity
authority exists.

It does not authorize missing-fraction arithmetic.

---

## 1. Existing semantic outcome

`missing_fraction` remains a recognized closed method identity.

Current S1-S3 authority does not establish:

- explicit expected opportunity identities;
- a positive authoritative opportunity set;
- one-to-one observation/opportunity fulfillment;
- duplicate-opportunity semantics;
- authoritative opportunity scope/window identity.

Therefore R2 must not infer missing opportunities from row count, cadence,
absence, timestamps, or acquisition frequency.

Until a separate opportunity contract is authorized, the ordinary result is:

`MISSING_OPPORTUNITY_AUTHORITY`

---

## 2. Closed method-layer accounting reason

The following closed R2 MethodEvidenceUse exclusion reason is accepted:

`method:missing_opportunity_authority`

When the configured method is exactly `missing_fraction` and no accepted
opportunity authority exists, every structurally valid considered observation
MAY be accounted as:

- role: `EXCLUDED`
- reason: `method:missing_opportunity_authority`

This reason records only that observation evidence cannot establish the
required opportunity denominator or one-to-one fulfillment relation.

It does not assert that the observation itself is invalid, stale, conflicting,
or otherwise unusable for another method.

---

## 3. Required conditions

R2 may emit `method:missing_opportunity_authority` only when:

1. the configuration identifies `missing_fraction`;
2. configuration and field-contract identity validation has succeeded;
3. authoritative S2/S3 input structure has passed R2 validation;
4. no accepted opportunity artifact is available;
5. the result is exactly
   `MethodNoResultReason.MISSING_OPPORTUNITY_AUTHORITY`;
6. no numeric missing-fraction value is produced.

---

## 4. Forbidden behavior

This reason MUST NOT:

- infer expected opportunities from observation count;
- infer expected cadence;
- infer absent acquisition events;
- infer one observation equals one opportunity;
- accept a caller-supplied denominator;
- synthesize opportunity IDs;
- authorize duplicate-opportunity handling;
- authorize arithmetic;
- construct an `AuthoritativeAnalyticalSet`;
- select providers;
- resolve conflicts;
- alter admission, identity, continuity, or conflict artifacts;
- act as a placeholder for implementation progress.

Malformed authoritative artifacts remain typed errors and MUST NOT be
converted into `MISSING_OPPORTUNITY_AUTHORITY`.

---

## 5. Evidence accounting

For a structurally valid `missing_fraction` request without opportunity
authority:

- every considered observation is recorded exactly once;
- every evidence-use entry is `EXCLUDED`;
- the closed reason is
  `method:missing_opportunity_authority`;
- an empty considered population yields an empty evidence-use tuple;
- no observation is marked `USED`.

The accounting reason does not claim current or historical support eligibility.

---

## 6. Determinism

`method:missing_opportunity_authority` is a closed constant.

It contains no:

- caller payload;
- timestamp;
- provider identity;
- random component;
- wall-clock dependency;
- ordering-dependent text.

Equivalent authoritative inputs therefore produce equivalent accounting.

---

## 7. Scope

This clarification applies only to:

- M3.2 S4-A;
- R2-B;
- `missing_fraction`;
- `MethodEvidenceUse.reason`;
- `MISSING_OPPORTUNITY_AUTHORITY`;
- the non-arithmetic `bind_method_support` boundary.

It does not authorize:

- R3 computation;
- missing-fraction arithmetic;
- opportunity inference;
- assessment;
- assembly;
- production;
- persistence.

---

## Acceptance

Authority decision:

`R2B_MISSING_OPPORTUNITY_REASON_AUTHORITY_CLARIFIED`

All other accepted S4-A, R2, and S3 semantics remain unchanged.
