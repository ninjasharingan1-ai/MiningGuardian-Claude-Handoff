# M3.2 ADR 001 — Field Assessment and Partial Snapshot Semantics

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Semantic decisions:** A, B, C, D, U  
**Acceptance:** ACCEPTED  
**Accepted by:** Human Owner  
**Acceptance date:** 2026-09-20

## Context

The frozen [M3 data contracts](../M3_DATA_CONTRACTS.md) identify `EstimatedState` as an inferred current condition with source observation references, method identity, freshness, quality, and limitations. They do not choose per-field validity, confidence, uncertainty, or snapshot completeness rules. The exact [M3.2 semantic-resolution report](../reviews/M3.2_SEMANTIC_CONTRACT_RESOLUTION_REPORT.md), sections 4–9 and 25, supplies the proposed rules formalized here. The [M3.2 preflight](../reviews/M3.2_READ_ONLY_PREFLIGHT_REPORT.md), section 34, identified their absence as a handoff blocker.

## Problem

Miner, GPU, pool, and economic evidence can be independently available or unavailable. A single snapshot-wide usability flag would hide this distinction. A numeric confidence or uncertainty score without a defined basis would imply precision the current evidence cannot support.

## Decision

Assess each requested `EstimatedStateField` independently. Permit mixed-validity `StateSnapshot` artifacts. Use structured `StateConfidence` and dimensioned `StateUncertainty`. Every field has a versioned `FieldEvidenceContract` that separates minimum validity requirements from confidence-strengthening checks. Consumers declare their own required field subset and whether they accept degraded values.

## Detailed Semantics

### StateValidity and value invariants

| Status | Meaning | Current-value slot |
|---|---|---|
| `VALID` | Required evidence, identity, temporal, and method conditions pass without a qualifying degradation | Non-null |
| `DEGRADED` | A non-null value remains supported under an explicitly named permitted limitation or fallback | Non-null, with its restricted interpretation |
| `UNKNOWN` | Available evidence does not establish the requested value | Null |
| `INVALID` | Detected integrity, identity, dimensional, or method violation prevents a defensible value | Null, with a failure reason |

Missing, stale-only, or insufficient required evidence normally yields `UNKNOWN`. An unresolved conflict between plausible eligible values yields `UNKNOWN` by default. `INVALID` requires positive evidence of a violation; mere absence is insufficient. An issue affecting one field does not automatically invalidate independent fields. A rejected observation may coexist with a `VALID` field only if that field still has sufficient, unambiguous support under its declared contract. `DEGRADED` requires a named, versioned rule. A last-known or diagnostic value belongs outside the unavailable current-value slot. Zero remains a valid numeric value when supported and is never a missing-value sentinel.

### StateSnapshot completeness

The snapshot lists the requested field and subject scope. Requested-but-unavailable fields remain explicit; unrequested fields are distinguishable from them. Its summary is:

- `COMPLETE`: every requested field is `VALID`.
- `PARTIAL`: at least one requested field is `VALID` or `DEGRADED`, and `COMPLETE` does not apply.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: no requested field has a usable current value.

A structurally inconsistent snapshot, including duplicate field identities or inconsistent reference times, is a construction or load error rather than an ordinary completeness state. Summary counts and field-key lists must agree with the field records. An `UNAVAILABLE` snapshot may be retained for diagnosis. No universal `safe_to_use` flag exists; a consumer evaluates its required subset and its tolerance for `DEGRADED` values.

### StateConfidence

`StateConfidence` means strength and completeness of support for this particular estimated value under its declared method and assumptions. It records `interpretation = CURRENT_ESTIMATE_SUPPORT`, a rubric ID/version, support status, individually identified checks, reason codes, limitations, and supporting references. Check results are `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNKNOWN`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`.

- `SUPPORTED`: all applicable support checks pass.
- `LIMITED`: the minimum estimation requirements pass, while a strengthening check fails or remains unknown.
- `UNASSESSED`: no value exists, or the method/evidence cannot assess support.

A `VALID` field can have `LIMITED` confidence if optional corroboration is absent. Confidence is neither a probability nor M2 `DecisionConfidenceResult`, observation quality, freshness, evidence reliability, or authentication. The initial contract has no numeric aggregate and no arbitrary HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW labels.

### StateUncertainty

Assessment dimensions are `measurement`, `temporal`, `source_disagreement`, `evidence_incompleteness`, `identity`, and `method`. Each carries a status, reason, and evidence references:

- `QUANTIFIED`: an explicit magnitude, interval, or other accepted quantitative representation exists, with unit, method/version, assumptions, and coverage interpretation where applicable.
- `KNOWN_UNQUANTIFIED`: a particular limitation is established, but its magnitude is unavailable. The quantitative value is null and the limitation is also recorded separately.
- `UNKNOWN`: even characterization is unsupported.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: nonapplicability is justified by the field definition.

The frozen glossary uses *uncertainty* for quantified lack of certainty. `KNOWN_UNQUANTIFIED` is an assessment status, not a fabricated numeric uncertainty value. Strong confidence support can coexist with a known limitation; a usable field can retain uncertainty. Unknown uncertainty is not zero. Missing evidence and uncertainty remain separate. No opaque aggregate uncertainty score is introduced.

### FieldEvidenceContract

Each field declares and versions its estimand/subject scope; required and optional source signals; source semantics and identity; units/conversions; admissible quality; temporal basis and bounded window; sample/span and coverage/gap minima; continuity; conflict rule; permitted degradation; confidence rubric; output domain; and resource limits. It records required, satisfied, unavailable, conflicting, and optional evidence requirements separately.

The mathematical minimum is one eligible sample for mean/median/min/max, two ordered samples for delta, two distinct eligible times with positive elapsed time for rate/slope, and an explicit denominator for missing fraction. These are not universal sufficiency thresholds; a field may require more. Deployment-dependent windows and thresholds are explicit, retained configuration. Missing required configuration is an error, not permission to invent a default. A failed minimum yields `UNKNOWN`; a declared permissible shortfall yields `DEGRADED`; a failed optional strengthening check limits confidence; unresolved effect magnitude remains uncertainty. Reasons remain in limitations and provenance.

## Invariants

`StateValidity`, `StateConfidence`, `StateUncertainty`, observation quality, and freshness are distinct assessments. A snapshot summary never erases field-level detail. An estimated value requires supporting observation references and an explicit method. Field validity grants neither M2 Policy admissibility nor execution permission. `EstimatedState` is never serialized as raw `RuntimeObservation`.

## Rejected Alternatives

One global snapshot-valid boolean; zero as a missing sentinel; unjustified numeric/probabilistic confidence; arbitrary HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW confidence; one opaque uncertainty score; a universal evidence minimum; silent degradation; and automatic promotion of legacy M2 `ContextValidity` or decision confidence.

## Consequences

Estimated fields require explicit assessment metadata. Snapshots may mix all four validity states. Consumers must state their required subset and degraded-value policy. State-confidence logic cannot reuse M2 decision-confidence semantics. Missing economic evidence need not erase independently supported miner or GPU state.

## Compatibility

This ADR does not modify M2 Evidence → Claim → Hypothesis → Candidate semantics; ADR-0006 scoring; `DecisionPolicy`, `DecisionScorer`, or `DecisionRanker` ownership; M2 Prediction or DecisionConfidence semantics; M2 persistence reconstruction; M3.1 `RuntimeObservation`, timestamp, quality, freshness, or runtime-evidence authority; or the M2/M3 no-execution boundary. The frozen M3.0 ownership split remains intact.

## Persistence Impact

When a state is persisted under ADR 003, its fields, validity, confidence, uncertainty, completeness, limitations, evidence-contract versions, and requested-field scope must round-trip unchanged. Loading does not reassess them. This ADR does not choose tables or storage technology.

## Observability / Evidence Impact

Supporting, unavailable, conflicting, and excluded observations remain traceable. Raw M3.1 observations retain their original quality and freshness; field assessment is a separately labeled derived interpretation. Last-known context never fills an `UNKNOWN` current-value slot.

## Verification Requirements

Future tests must cover mixed-validity and entirely unavailable snapshots; exact `COMPLETE`/`PARTIAL`/`UNAVAILABLE` rules; requested versus unrequested fields; nullability and zero; independent source failures; failed minimum versus optional support checks; `VALID` with `LIMITED` confidence; all uncertainty dimensions and statuses; unsupported numeric probability claims; and field-level persistence round-trip. Test boundaries for sample count, span, denominator, and missing required configuration. No implementation tests run during ADR drafting.

## Security / Trust Implications

Future implementation must test poisoned but syntactically valid evidence, identity/unit mismatch, false corroboration, and attempts to promote quality or freshness into confidence. An apparently valid observation is not proof that its source is truthful. None of these assessments grants control authority.

## Implementation Boundary

This ADR does not authorize M3.2 implementation by itself.

Implementation begins only after:
1. all required M3.2 ADRs are ACCEPTED;
2. ADR acceptance closure and integrity verification are complete;
3. `M3.2_IMPLEMENTATION_HANDOFF` is prepared;
4. implementation is explicitly authorized.
