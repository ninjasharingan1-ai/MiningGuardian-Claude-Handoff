# M3.2 S4A R2-B — Historical Support Reason Authority Clarification

## Status

`R2B_HISTORICAL_REASON_AUTHORITY_CLARIFIED`

ACCEPTED NORMATIVE CLARIFICATION.

This clarification closes only the MethodEvidenceUse accounting gap for
historical R2-B support binding.

It does not alter S3 admission, identity, continuity, or conflict semantics.

---

## 1. Existing historical outcomes remain authoritative

Historical methods remain:

- `within_segment_delta`
- `source_time_counter_rate`
- `historical_endpoint_slope`

Historical support requires:

- historical admission eligibility;
- exact subject identity;
- field/signal compatibility;
- exact unit compatibility;
- authoritative conflict/comparability handling;
- one authoritative sufficient continuity segment.

Existing ordinary no-result outcomes remain unchanged:

- no historical evidence:
  `NO_ELIGIBLE_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE`

- multiple support sets without one authoritative comparable set:
  `NO_SINGLE_COMPARABLE_ANALYTICAL_SET`

- no one authoritative sufficient continuity segment:
  `NO_SINGLE_CONTINUITY_SEGMENT`

- unresolved full-population conflict:
  `UNRESOLVED_CONFLICT`

---

## 2. Artifact-derived reasons retain precedence

Accepted upstream reasons remain preferred whenever they apply:

- `admission:<outcome>:<primary_reason>`
- `identity:<component>:<reason>`
- `continuity:<reason>:<detail>`
- `conflict:<kind>:<reason>`
- `not_compared:<reason>`
- `field_signal_out_of_scope`
- `unit_conversion_not_authorized`

The method-layer reasons below may be used only when no more specific
accepted artifact-derived exclusion reason applies.

---

## 3. Historical comparable-set accounting

The already accepted closed reason:

`method:no_single_comparable_analytical_set`

is extended narrowly to the historical-support view.

R2 MAY emit this reason for an otherwise eligible historical observation
only when all of the following hold:

1. the closed method maps to `HISTORICAL`;
2. the observation is otherwise eligible;
3. multiple eligible support streams remain;
4. no full-population authoritative S3 agreement authorizes one analytical set;
5. no unresolved full-population conflict supplies a more specific
   conflict-derived exclusion reason;
6. the R2 outcome is exactly
   `NO_SINGLE_COMPARABLE_ANALYTICAL_SET`;
7. no more specific accepted artifact-derived reason applies.

This reason MUST NOT:

- authorize joining streams;
- merge partial agreement groups;
- select a provider;
- select a stream;
- rank evidence;
- override a conflict;
- replace a more specific artifact-derived reason.

---

## 4. Historical continuity accounting

The following closed R2 method-layer exclusion reason is accepted:

`method:no_single_continuity_segment`

R2 MAY emit this reason for an otherwise eligible historical observation
only when all of the following hold:

1. the closed method maps to `HISTORICAL`;
2. admission, identity, field/signal, unit, and applicable comparability
   requirements have already been satisfied;
3. the authoritative continuity result does not establish one sufficient
   segment covering the intended historical analytical support;
4. R2 does not choose a segment by UUID order, provider name, stream name,
   caller order, latest ingestion, or any hidden ranking;
5. the R2 outcome is exactly
   `NO_SINGLE_CONTINUITY_SEGMENT`;
6. no more specific accepted continuity-derived or other artifact-derived
   exclusion reason applies.

This includes ordinary cases such as:

- eligible evidence spans more than one authoritative segment;
- the only candidate segment is explicitly insufficient for derivation;
- eligible evidence cannot be placed into one shared sufficient segment.

---

## 5. Forbidden continuity behavior

`method:no_single_continuity_segment` MUST NOT:

- fabricate a continuity boundary;
- replace an existing specific continuity artifact reason;
- select one of multiple segments;
- join segments;
- cross a validated boundary;
- authorize arithmetic;
- repair insufficient evidence;
- infer restart or incarnation;
- depend on UUID lexical ordering;
- depend on caller ordering.

---

## 6. Evidence accounting

For `MethodNoResult`:

- every considered observation remains `EXCLUDED`;
- existing artifact-derived exclusion reasons are preserved;
- otherwise eligible observations blocked only by comparable-set authority
  may use:
  `method:no_single_comparable_analytical_set`;
- otherwise eligible observations blocked only by continuity authority
  may use:
  `method:no_single_continuity_segment`.

No caller-supplied free-form reason is authorized.

---

## 7. Determinism

Both method-layer reasons are closed constants.

They contain:

- no caller payload;
- no provider identity;
- no timestamp;
- no wall-clock input;
- no random value;
- no list-order dependency.

Equivalent authoritative inputs must therefore produce equivalent
historical support-binding outcomes.

---

## 8. Scope

This clarification applies only to:

- M3.2 S4-A;
- R2-B;
- historical support binding;
- `MethodEvidenceUse.reason`;
- `NO_SINGLE_COMPARABLE_ANALYTICAL_SET`;
- `NO_SINGLE_CONTINUITY_SEGMENT`.

It does not authorize:

- R3 arithmetic;
- endpoint calculation;
- rate calculation;
- slope calculation;
- conversion arithmetic;
- assessment;
- assembly;
- production;
- persistence;
- missing-fraction opportunity semantics;
- provider ranking;
- hidden conflict resolution.

---

## Acceptance

Authority decision:

`R2B_HISTORICAL_REASON_AUTHORITY_CLARIFIED`

This clarification extends only the minimum R2 evidence-accounting authority
required to express already-authorized historical no-result outcomes.

All other accepted R2 and S3 semantics remain unchanged.
