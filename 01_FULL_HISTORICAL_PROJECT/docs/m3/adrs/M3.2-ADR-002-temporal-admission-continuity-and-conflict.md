# M3.2 ADR 002 — Temporal Admission, Continuity and Conflict Semantics

**Status:** ACCEPTED  
**Date:** 2026-09-20  
**Semantic decisions:** F, G, H, I, W  
**Acceptance:** ACCEPTED  
**Accepted by:** Human Owner  
**Acceptance date:** 2026-09-20

## Context

The frozen [M3 data contracts](../M3_DATA_CONTRACTS.md) and [runtime evidence contract](../M3_RUNTIME_EVIDENCE.md) keep observation, state, and provenance distinct. M3.1 records source time and Guardian receipt time separately, including a null source time. The exact [M3.2 semantic-resolution report](../reviews/M3.2_SEMANTIC_CONTRACT_RESOLUTION_REPORT.md), sections 10–14 and 26, specifies temporal admission, conflicts, stale context, and continuity. The [preflight](../reviews/M3.2_READ_ONLY_PREFLIGHT_REPORT.md) identified the missing operational rules.

## Problem

Different clocks and cadences cannot be made simultaneous by sharing a receipt or acquisition ID. Unresolved source disagreement cannot be hidden by selecting a winner. Old readings and counter values across resets cannot safely describe an unqualified current state or rate.

## Decision

Evaluate every field against an explicit, caller-supplied aware-UTC `state_reference_time` (`T`) and a field-specific finite window `(T − W, T]`. Keep source-recording and Guardian-receipt bases distinct. Represent comparable disagreement as `StateConflict`; initially, no general cross-source resolver exists. Stale-only evidence leaves the current-value field `UNKNOWN`, with separately labeled historical context. Calculate counter deltas and rates only within reproducible analytical continuity segments. Apply the same non-silent conflict principle to future economic providers.

## Detailed Semantics

### Reference time and temporal admission

`T` is the instant whose current state is estimated, not observation time, ingestion time, or production time. Every field in one snapshot uses the same `T`, while its own support interval and source timestamps remain visible. A historical replay supplies its historical `T` explicitly; estimation does not call the wall clock. `W` is positive, finite, field-specific configuration. Exact source timestamp equality is not required.

An observation's `observation_time` is source recording time; `ingestion_time` is Guardian receipt time. Source time may be null. Source-time current estimation requires the declared clock, age, quality, and method conditions. Receipt-only evidence may support a clearly labeled *received/last-reported descriptive context*, but receipt time never substitutes for unknown source measurement time. In particular, the present M3.1 NVML and miner observations with no established source recording clock cannot thereby assert physical measurement age. `event_time` is used only when the field contract explicitly gives that distinct source event a role.

As-of reconstruction excludes evidence received after `T`; a recorded processing time after `T` also precludes claiming the observation was then canonicalized. Missing processing time does not prove that a historical decision consumed the evidence. Future or inconsistent clocks are excluded from current support, with reasons retained; no future-time clamp is inherited from legacy behavior. Delayed or out-of-order evidence may still serve an eligible historical window under its field method. Its original M3.1 quality and freshness are never rewritten by M3.2 admission.

Cross-source comparison requires proven subject identity, compatible quantity and units, an applicable effective-time interpretation, and an explicit maximum alignment difference. Receipt proximity and a shared acquisition/correlation ID do not prove physical simultaneity. Temporal misalignment is recorded separately from value disagreement. Query transport order is not source event order.

### StateConflict and initial resolver policy

`StateConflict` records `conflict_id`, affected field key, conflict kind, all participant evidence references, comparison contract ID/version and basis, resolution status, and reason. Optional resolution-rule ID/version, selected/rejected references, and quantified disagreement are retained when applicable. Kinds include `VALUE_DISAGREEMENT`, `IDENTITY_MISMATCH`, `TEMPORAL_MISALIGNMENT`, and `UNIT_OR_SEMANTIC_MISMATCH`. Compare values only when subject, quantity, units, and effective interval are demonstrably compatible. Missing evidence and source failure are not disagreement.

There is initially **no general cross-source winner**: no latest-wins, preferred-source, averaging, majority vote, maximum/minimum rule, or hidden provider ranking. For comparable conflicting eligible values, an unresolved conflict makes the *current field* `UNKNOWN` and preserves all participants. A positive integrity or identity violation may instead make it `INVALID`. A `DEGRADED` estimate from conflict needs a separately accepted, explicit, versioned field-specific resolution rule. Chronological selection *within one verified stream* is possible only as a named estimator; it is not cross-source precedence. Two raw hashrates or other numbers with different meanings are not treated as comparable merely because their units match.

For economic providers, keep provider-specific observations before promotion. Comparison additionally requires matching network/asset/market or pool scope and source semantics. Evaluate each candidate's quality and freshness separately. One eligible candidate without an unresolved comparable contradiction may be promoted under its field contract; contradictory eligible candidates remain conflicted with a null canonical value. Agreement does not prove independence of shared upstream sources. No provider is silently averaged or ranked.

### Stale, missing, and last-known evidence

Stale-only evidence cannot populate a current-value field. It can remain in a separate, explicitly historical/last-known context containing the value, observation reference, source time if known, receipt time, and age assessment. An `UNKNOWN` current value does not erase that history. Older evidence can contribute to a descriptive historical window if it lies within that window. A statistic claiming current state must also satisfy declared terminal-recency, coverage, and method requirements. New snapshot production never refreshes the age of old inputs. A newer explicit failure/invalidation cannot be hidden by silently selecting an older success.

There is no generic stale-value `DEGRADED` fallback and no implicit forward fill, interpolation, or imputation. A later field-specific fallback needs its own accepted method contract. Missing evidence and stale evidence have different reasons and provenance.

### Continuity and counter-derived features

A continuity segment is an **analytical evidence segment**, not a miner process incarnation. Its provenance includes source/source instance, signal, subject and workload/algorithm scope, ordering basis, segment anchor/boundary evidence, known incarnation only if proven, and segmentation method/version. Break it on an applicable explicit incarnation change, counter decrease/reset, recorded discontinuity, subject/workload/algorithm or counter-semantic change, excessive configured gap, unresolved order conflict, or identity conflict. A counter or uptime decrease alone does not prove a restart.

Never compute delta or rate across a segment boundary. A new segment must meet its own sample and elapsed-time minimum. Do not clamp negative deltas, assume rollover, or infer a physical share-production rate from receipt-only timing. Where the source semantics permit, a segment-local rate means observed change in that source counter per known elapsed source time; it does not automatically prove credited work. Unknown process incarnation remains explicitly unknown. M3.1 process-local continuity history is diagnostic evidence, not authority for deterministic reconstruction.

## Invariants

Source time ≠ receipt time. Stale ≠ current. Missing ≠ stale. Source failure ≠ source disagreement. Receipt proximity ≠ physical simultaneity. Shared acquisition ID ≠ physical simultaneity. Uptime decrease ≠ proved miner restart. Counter decrease ≠ proved miner restart. Every exclusion, conflict, and continuity boundary remains traceable; no input is silently relabeled fresh or valid.

## Rejected Alternatives

Exact timestamp equality; implicit receipt-time substitution; legacy future-time clamping; one universal window; a cross-source latest/preferred/highest/lowest/average/majority winner; hidden economic-provider ranking; stale carry-forward into current value; cross-reset arithmetic; and process-restart inference from counter or uptime decrease.

## Consequences

Each field needs declared temporal basis, finite window, alignment tolerance where applicable, and continuity rule. Current fields can be `UNKNOWN` while historical context remains readable. Disagreement may reduce completeness under ADR 001 without removing independent fields. Future conflict-resolution policies require explicit accepted versions. No current economic value is promoted through an undocumented provider-selection policy.

## Compatibility

This ADR does not modify M2 Evidence → Claim → Hypothesis → Candidate semantics; ADR-0006 scoring; `DecisionPolicy`, `DecisionScorer`, or `DecisionRanker` ownership; M2 Prediction or DecisionConfidence semantics; M2 persistence reconstruction; M3.1 `RuntimeObservation`, timestamp, quality, freshness, or runtime-evidence authority; or the M2/M3 no-execution boundary. The frozen M3.0 ownership split remains intact. `StateValidity` follows ADR 001 rather than redefining M2 admissibility.

## Persistence Impact

Derived artifacts under ADR 003 preserve `T`, each support window and temporal basis, conflict participants and resolution state, stale-context labeling, and continuity-segment provenance. Loading restores these assessments; it does not recalculate or overwrite raw M3.1 observations.

## Observability / Evidence Impact

M3.1 source clock nullability, receipt time, quality, freshness, correlation, and source identity remain raw evidence. M3.2 admission records why each observation was included or excluded. Acquisition correlation remains grouping evidence, not causality or physical simultaneity. Provider-specific evidence stays identifiable even when no canonical economic value can be promoted.

## Verification Requirements

Future tests must cover explicit historical `T`; missing source clocks; future/inconsistent event and recording times; receipt-after-`T` and known processing-after-`T`; delayed and out-of-order evidence; cross-source clock skew and shared acquisition ID; stale-only and later-failure cases; zero versus missing; comparable and noncomparable disagreements; source failure distinct from disagreement; counter resets, uptime decreases, long gaps, unknown incarnation, and no cross-segment rate; economic-provider disagreement and no hidden winner. Test original M3.1 quality/freshness immutability. No implementation tests run during ADR drafting.

## Security / Trust Implications

Future implementation must test poisoned but syntactically valid observations, timestamp manipulation, spoofed identity, unit mismatch, conflict suppression, malicious provider disagreement, and forged continuity claims. Neither proximity nor an acquisition ID authenticates a physical event. This ADR grants no control authority.

## Implementation Boundary
